## Unlocking the app

Keep a device passcode enabled. TripDocket uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode as available. If authentication fails or is cancelled, the app stays locked and you can try again. Support cannot bypass the device lock. There is no separate TripDocket account password to reset.

## Import and offline checks

PDF, JPEG and PNG originals are supported. If a file is in cloud storage, finish downloading it in Files before importing. A thumbnail alone does not establish that the original is available.

Open a saved original and run the original-file check before travel, including an Airplane Mode check. The check reads the entire saved file; it does not confirm visa eligibility, booking validity or sufficient travel documents. If a saved original is damaged or missing, preserve your available files and recover from a known-good backup. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step.

## Limits and Pro

Free permits one trip, two people and eight documents in the current library. Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It adds more entries, batch import of up to 10 files, and reminders you explicitly enable. Family Sharing for Pro is not part of this offer; there is no collaborative sync.

All tiers have a 16 MiB limit per original and a 64 MiB total for originals. An incoming backup must be at most 96 MiB. The interface labels these values 16/64/96 MB. Pro does not increase those size limits. Original checks, viewing, editing, deletion, basic export and backup recovery remain available without Pro.

For missing Pro, use Restore purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen with the Apple Account used for the purchase. A connection and Apple authentication may be needed. The price displayed on the Pro screen comes from the App Store. A pending or cancelled purchase does not unlock Pro. Restoring a purchase recovers the license only, not files.

## Several files at once

With Pro, open a trip's actions and choose Import multiple files. Choose at most 10 originals. Review and confirm every item, then save the batch. Editing a confirmed item requires confirmation again. If preparation fails, no part is added; correct the file or choose fewer files. Keep the app process running while reviewing; quitting discards the unsaved draft.

## Export and recovery

Export a chosen packet or a full backup before deleting the app or replacing a library. Exports are readable and unencrypted. Choose a trusted destination; copies already sent cannot be recalled.

Restore from backup accepts a .tripbackup file. After confirmation it replaces the entire local library, not merges it. Export the existing library first if you need both sets. An invalid backup is rejected before replacement. If the device and every backup are lost, support cannot reconstruct the originals.

## Reminders

Enable a reminder only after checking the expiry date. Notification permission and device settings affect delivery. Reminder text is generic. The reminder is scheduled for 9 AM in the device time zone in use when you schedule it, normally 30 days before expiry, or the next 9 AM before the expiry day when inside that window. It does not automatically follow a later time-zone change. Document edits or a replacement restore remove affected schedules; set them again if needed. Reminders are a convenience, not travel-rule advice.
