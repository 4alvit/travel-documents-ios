## Documents on your device

TripDocket keeps a local library of the originals you import or scan, and the details you enter: trips, people, document titles, types and optional dates. It also keeps file-integrity information and the time of the last original-file check. Names can be nicknames; a legal name is not required.

Document reading, integrity checks and optional text recognition run on the device. The reviewed app does not operate a developer-hosted document server or automatically upload the library to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-upload SDK and does not require a TripDocket account. These statements describe the application; they do not mean that Apple services, an export destination or a support website process no data.

## Permissions and Apple services

The camera is used when you choose to scan a document. Files import reads the files you select in the system picker. You can manage camera permission in iOS Settings. If a chosen file is stored by a cloud provider, that provider may need to download it before import.

The app uses Apple's device-owner authentication to unlock its interface. A device passcode is required. The app receives the authentication result, not the device passcode or a face/fingerprint template. The local library uses the iOS sandbox and complete file protection. This is not a separate encrypted vault password and is not a guarantee against access on a compromised device.

Notification permission is requested when you explicitly enable a reminder. The scheduled notification uses generic text without a person's name or document title. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings. Reminder dates are the dates you confirm.

Purchases and restoring purchases use Apple's StoreKit. The app reads product information and verified purchase-entitlement information to decide whether Pro is active. It does not receive payment-card details. Apple processes purchases and its other services under its own terms and privacy policy.

Standard device backup may include the library according to your Apple settings. TripDocket has no collaborative cloud-sync service. Manage device backups through Apple's settings and services.

## Copies you choose to share

Export and sharing are actions you choose. Trip packets and backups contain readable, unencrypted document originals and associated details. A recipient, storage provider or another app with access to a copy can read it. Sharing from the original preview likewise sends a copy of that original. TripDocket cannot recall copies you have shared or control how another service stores them.

A selected packet contains the selected documents and referenced trips/people; a full backup also includes empty trips and people. Restoring a backup replaces the current local library after confirmation. It does not merge libraries.

## Retention and deletion

Saved originals and details stay in the local library until you delete them, replace the library by restoring a backup, or remove the app's data. Deleting a trip also deletes its documents from the active library; deleting a person leaves their documents unassigned. Export a backup first if you need to keep a copy. We do not promise forensic erasure of device storage.

Unsaved batch-import files are held in memory while you review them and are not a saved backup. Quitting the process discards that draft. Preview/share operations create protected temporary files, excluded from device backup, which the app cleans after the applicable operation or during later cleanup. Cleanup may be delayed when a share extension is active or a file operation fails; it is not a promise of immediate erasure.

Deleting inside TripDocket does not delete exports, recipient copies or existing system backups. Manage those separately. Losing the device and every backup can mean losing the library; Restore Purchases cannot recover documents.
